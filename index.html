About Me

I’m a graphic designer with over three years of experience helping startup businesses build strong and memorable brand identities. I’m a passionate and adaptable creative with a growing background in graphic design and digital content creation, focused on transforming ideas into clean, visually compelling designs. About Me

The Journey

My journey in graphic design started with a strong desire to turn ideas into meaningful visual experiences. In 2023, I completed a Graphic Design Bootcamp and further expanded my creative foundation through studies with the California Institute of the Arts. These experiences helped me develop a deeper understanding of branding, visual communication, and digital creativity.

As I continued growing my skills through hands on projects and continuous learning, I began working independently as a freelance graphic designer and illustrator, helping brands and startups create clean, engaging, and professional visual identities. In 2025, I worked as a Visual Designer at RICHSEA TOKEN, where I gained more experience creating visual assets and digital brand materials in a fast paced creative environment. Through each stage of my journey, I’ve remained passionate about creating designs that not only look good but also communicate purpose and value effectively.
